## Summary

GVE Level 3 Evidence and Result Realization defines evidence identity, attribution, provenance, freshness, integrity, effect-claim state separation, uncertainty, sufficiency, contradiction handling, operation-result realization, authoritative workflow-result assembly, fail-closed finalization, and immutable finalized results without defining maintained runtime, lifecycle, or plugin-specific evidence implementation.

## Definitions

### Evidence record (`L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-RECORD`)

An immutable, uniquely identified and attributable record of one observation, inspection, measurement, response, artifact, or other fact used to evaluate one or more exact effect claims.

### Evidence provenance (`L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE`)

The complete attribution of evidence to its producer, collection method, observation context, governing authority, workflow, operation, attempt, plugin, action, observation time, and integrity facts.

### Evidence freshness boundary (`L3-ERR-FRESHNESS-BOUNDARY`)

The explicit temporal or version boundary within which one evidence record is valid for one exact effect claim and one result-realization attempt.

### Effect-claim state (`L3-ERR-EFFECT-CLAIM-STATE`)

The application-independent state of one exact effect claim, preserving distinct requested, authorized, attempted, completed, observed, and verified facts.

### Uncertainty state (`L3-ERR-UNCERTAINTY-STATE`)

The explicit representation that one fact or effect claim is unknown, unavailable, not observed, contradicted, or supported by insufficient evidence.

### Evidence sufficiency (`L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-SUFFICIENCY`)

The fail-closed determination that a complete attributable evidence set satisfies the exact governing requirements for one effect claim without unresolved conflict, ambiguity, staleness, or uncertainty.

### Evidence contradiction (`L3-ERR-CONTRADICTION`)

Two or more attributable evidence records or claims that cannot simultaneously be accepted as true under the same governing authority and realization context.

### Realized operation result (`L3-ERR-OPERATION-RESULT`)

The uniquely identified result bound to one workflow, one operation, one accepted-plan snapshot, one validated operation contract, the complete set of operation attempts, and the complete accepted evidence and uncertainty set.

### Authoritative workflow result (`L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`)

The complete uniquely identified assembly containing exactly one realized result for every operation in the accepted workflow plan and preserving all partial, failed, blocked, skipped, cancelled, timed-out, interrupted, unattempted, and unresolved conditions.

### Result finalization (`L3-ERR-RESULT-FINALIZATION`)

The single fail-closed determination that one authoritative workflow result is complete, internally consistent, sufficiently evidenced, uniquely attributable, and immutable.

## Normative requirements

### L3-ERR-REQ-001

Every evidence record must have one stable identity and be uniquely attributable to the workflow, operation, attempt, plugin, action, governing authority, evidence producer, collection method, observation context, and observation time.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-RECORD`, `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE`

### L3-ERR-REQ-002

Evidence provenance must explicitly identify the collection method, producer, governing authority, observation time, freshness boundary, source context, and integrity facts required to evaluate authenticity and applicability.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE`, `L3-ERR-FRESHNESS-BOUNDARY`

### L3-ERR-REQ-003

Evidence accepted into one result-realization attempt must remain immutable; replacement, mutation, deletion, or reinterpretation after acceptance must create new attributable evidence or fail closed.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-RECORD`, `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE`

### L3-ERR-REQ-004

Requested, authorized, attempted, completed, observed, and verified effect-claim states must remain distinct, and none may be inferred from workflow or operation lifecycle status alone.

References: `L3-ERR-EFFECT-CLAIM-STATE`, `L3-WPL-LIFECYCLE-STATE`

### L3-ERR-REQ-005

Unknown, unavailable, not-observed, contradicted, and insufficient-evidence states must remain explicit and must not be converted into success, failure, completion, observation, or verification without sufficient attributable evidence.

References: `L3-ERR-UNCERTAINTY-STATE`, `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-SUFFICIENCY`

### L3-ERR-REQ-006

Evidence sufficiency must be evaluated against the exact effect claim, governing authority, accepted operation contract, freshness boundary, and required observation context rather than against generic lifecycle completion or executor success.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-SUFFICIENCY`, `L3-ERR-FRESHNESS-BOUNDARY`, `L3-PAC-VALIDATED-OPERATION-CONTRACT`

### L3-ERR-REQ-007

Duplicate, stale, ambiguous, unauthorized, malformed, conflicting, contradictory, integrity-invalid, or non-uniquely attributable evidence must fail closed and remain represented as unresolved or insufficient rather than silently selected or discarded.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-RECORD`, `L3-ERR-CONTRADICTION`, `L3-ERR-UNCERTAINTY-STATE`

### L3-ERR-REQ-008

Every realized operation result must have one stable identity and bind exactly one workflow identity, one operation identity, one accepted-plan snapshot identity, one validated operation contract, the complete set of created attempts, and the complete accepted evidence and uncertainty set.

References: `L3-ERR-OPERATION-RESULT`, `L3-WPL-ACCEPTED-PLAN-SNAPSHOT`, `L3-PAC-VALIDATED-OPERATION-CONTRACT`

### L3-ERR-REQ-009

Operation-result realization must account for every relevant lifecycle fact, effect claim, evidence record, uncertainty state, contradiction, insufficiency, and unresolved condition without collapsing distinct facts or omitting adverse evidence.

References: `L3-ERR-OPERATION-RESULT`, `L3-ERR-EFFECT-CLAIM-STATE`, `L3-ERR-UNCERTAINTY-STATE`, `L3-ERR-CONTRADICTION`

### L3-ERR-REQ-010

An authoritative workflow result must contain exactly one realized operation result for every operation in the accepted workflow plan and must preserve partial, failed, blocked, skipped, cancelled, timed-out, interrupted, unattempted, and unresolved conditions.

References: `L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`, `L3-ERR-OPERATION-RESULT`, `L3-WPL-COMPLETE-WORKFLOW-PLAN`

### L3-ERR-REQ-011

Workflow-result assembly must fail closed on missing, duplicate, conflicting, stale, ambiguous, non-unique, or operation-mismatched results and must not finalize an incomplete or internally inconsistent result set.

References: `L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`, `L3-ERR-RESULT-FINALIZATION`

### L3-ERR-REQ-012

Result finalization must be one deterministic fail-closed decision over the complete workflow result, including all operation results, lifecycle facts, effect claims, evidence, uncertainty, contradictions, and unresolved conditions.

References: `L3-ERR-RESULT-FINALIZATION`, `L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`

### L3-ERR-REQ-013

A finalized authoritative workflow result and every finalized operation result must remain immutable; correction or supersession must create a new uniquely attributable result version rather than mutate accepted result history.

References: `L3-ERR-RESULT-FINALIZATION`, `L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`, `L3-ERR-OPERATION-RESULT`

### L3-ERR-REQ-014

No operation result or workflow result may claim success, completion, observation, verification, publication, remote effect, or durable state without sufficient fresh uniquely attributable evidence for the exact claim.

References: `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-SUFFICIENCY`, `L3-ERR-EFFECT-CLAIM-STATE`, `L3-ERR-WORKFLOW-RESULT`

### L3-ERR-REQ-015

Lifecycle eligibility, attempt creation, blocking, skipping, cancellation, timeout, interruption, failure, completion, retry, and terminality must remain lifecycle facts and must not substitute for evidence or effect-claim realization.

References: `L3-WPL-LIFECYCLE-STATE`, `L3-ERR-EFFECT-CLAIM-STATE`, `L3-ERR-EVIDENCE-RECORD`
